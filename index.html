About Me

Jie’s research focuses on utilizing Genome-Wide Association Studies (GWAS) to uncover the interconnections and underlying mechanisms among complex diseases. By elucidating the biological mechanisms that link these diseases, he aims to facilitate disease prevention and early intervention. Furthermore, he aspires to establish and promote a novel research paradigm for studying disease comorbidity.
