About me

From pharmaceutical science to the genetics of complex disease.

I am a pharmaceutical science student developing research expertise in statistical genetics and computational biology. My work is motivated by a central question: how can inherited variation help us understand the biological connections between diseases?

I currently explore cancer–cardiovascular comorbidity using GWAS summary statistics, genetic correlation, conditional association and causal-inference methods. I value careful interpretation, transparent methods and biologically grounded questions.
