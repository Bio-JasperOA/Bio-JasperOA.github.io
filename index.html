About Me

Jie studies how embryonic cell states emerge, interact, and transition across time and space. He is currently rotating in Professor Xiaodong Liu’s Cellular Reprogramming and Developmental Regenerative Medicine Laboratory, where he is working on virtual embryo modelling. His research interests center on integrating single-cell and spatial transcriptomic data from natural human embryos with interpretable AI to reconstruct developmental trajectories, model cell-fate decisions, and understand tissue organization during human embryogenesis.

02

Research Interests

Virtual embryo

AI-based models of embryonic development that reconstruct and predict cell-state transitions across space and time.

Developmental dynamics

Temporal regulation of cell fate, lineage commitment, and tissue patterning during embryogenesis.

Single-cell & spatial omics

Integrating single-cell and spatial measurements to resolve cellular heterogeneity, lineage relationships, and tissue organization.

AI for developmental biology

Representation learning and generative modelling for multimodal integration and interpretable analysis of dynamic biological processes.
