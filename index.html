About Me

I am a pharmaceutical science student developing research expertise in statistical genetics and computational biology. I am interested in how inherited variation can explain biological connections between diseases.

My current work examines cancer–cardiovascular comorbidity using GWAS summary statistics, genetic correlation, conditional association and causal-inference methods.
