scFoundation: learning cell states at transcriptome scale

scFoundation combines transcriptome-scale pretraining with a read-depth-aware reconstruction task. This reading examines its architecture, quantitative evidence and practical value as a cell-state encoder, while separating representation learning from developmental simulation.
